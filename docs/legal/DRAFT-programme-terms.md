# Programme Terms

These terms describe how the **[PROGRAMME NAME]** runs, what we do, and what is
expected of you. They apply alongside the Terms of Enrolment and Payment you
accepted before payment. Please read them before setting your password and
entering the portal.

## 1. How the programme is structured

The programme runs in phases over approximately [DURATION]:

1. **Onboarding** — an introductory call with your Programme Associate, who is
   your point of contact throughout.
2. **Assessment** — two sessions with a Technical Associate that establish your
   current level. Session 1 is required. Session 2 is offered where Session 1
   indicates readiness for advanced problems.
3. **Batch placement** — you are placed in a batch matched to your assessed
   level, with the reason recorded and visible to you.
4. **Core programme** — scheduled sessions, assignments and mock assessments
   across [LIST PHASES].
5. **Review gate** — a review at the end of the [PHASE NAME] phase determines
   whether you continue to the next phase. The outcome and its reason are
   recorded and shared with you.
6. **Placement support** — described in section 6.

## 2. Assessment and batch placement

Your assessment outcome records the problems given, how many you solved, the
time taken, and a recommended level. You receive a summary written for you.
Internal notes made by the assessor are used for teaching decisions and are not
shared.

Your Programme Associate makes the final batch decision. Placement above the
assessor's recommendation requires a written justification, recorded against
your file.

## 3. What is expected of you

To progress through the programme you are expected to:

- attend at least **90%** of scheduled sessions;
- complete at least **85%** of assigned work;
- achieve at least **80%** across mock assessments;
- attend scheduled check-ins with your Programme Associate; and
- respond to reasonable contact from us within [N] working days.

These figures determine placement readiness. They are also the conditions
attached to the performance-based refund in your Terms of Enrolment and
Payment. Your current standing against each is visible to you in the portal.

## 4. Check-ins, support and raising a problem

Your Programme Associate logs a structured check-in with you on a [CADENCE]
basis, recording your progress, any query you raise, and how it was resolved.
You can see every note recorded about you.

If something is wrong, raise a ticket through the portal. Academic matters go
to your Programme Associate first. Conduct matters go directly to the Academic
Head. Fee and refund matters are compiled by your Programme Associate and
decided by the Academic Head, who is the only person authorised to decide them.

## 5. Falling behind, pausing and leaving

If your attendance or progress falls below the levels in section 3, or you
become unreachable, we will flag this and attempt to contact you up to three
times by call, WhatsApp and email. If we cannot reach you after three attempts,
your enrolment may be moved to **Paused** pending review.

You may also request a pause yourself, with a reason and an expected return
date. A pause is reviewed after [MAXIMUM PAUSE DURATION]. Time spent paused
[DOES / DOES NOT] extend the programme end date, and [DOES / DOES NOT] extend
any period referred to in the performance-based refund clause.

You may leave the programme at any time by written notice to [EMAIL].
Offboarding requires Academic Head approval and a recorded reason. Leaving does
not by itself create a right to a refund; refunds are governed solely by your
Terms of Enrolment and Payment.

## 6. Placement support

Once you reach Placement Active status, our Placement Coordinator shares
opportunities with you, recording the company, the role and the package
offered. You are expected to respond to each within [N] working days and to
attend interviews you have accepted.

Opportunities above [BRAND CEILING] are reviewed internally before being
shared. Declining opportunities without reason may affect your eligibility
under the performance-based refund clause.

We do not guarantee employment, a specific salary, a specific number of
interviews, or an offer from any employer.

## 7. Add-on tracks

Add-on tracks such as [LIST ADD-ONS] run alongside the main programme and are
tracked separately. Attendance and completion in an add-on do not count toward
the thresholds in section 3 unless stated otherwise at the time you enrol in
the add-on.

## 8. Conduct

You are expected to treat staff, mentors and other learners with respect, to
submit your own work, and not to share programme materials, assessment content
or recordings outside the programme. Material breach may result in offboarding
without refund, decided by the Academic Head.

## 9. Your account

Your account is personal to you. Do not share your login. You set your own
password; no member of our staff can see or set it, and nobody from the Company
will ever ask you for it.

## 10. Changes and record of acceptance

These terms apply in the version you accept. We record the version, the date
and time, and the IP address of your acceptance, and email you a copy. Revised
terms apply to enrolments made after they are published; they do not alter what
you agreed to.
